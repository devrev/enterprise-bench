# EXHIBIT A: SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

## Applicability

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") applies to all customers and defines Maple's uptime commitments, support response times, and remedies for SLA breaches. Specific metrics vary by Service Tier (Starter, Growth, or Enterprise).

---

## 1. PLATFORM UPTIME

### 1.1 Uptime Commitment

| Service Tier | Monthly Uptime Guarantee |
|--------------|-------------------------|
| Starter | 99.5% |
| Growth | 99.5% |
| Enterprise | 99.95% |

### 1.2 Uptime Calculation

**Uptime Percentage** = (Total Minutes in Month - Downtime Minutes) / Total Minutes in Month × 100

### 1.3 Exclusions from Downtime

The following are excluded from downtime calculations:
- Scheduled maintenance (with advance notice: 48 hours for Starter/Growth, 7 days for Enterprise)
- Customer-caused outages (e.g., incorrect API usage, account suspension)
- Force majeure events
- Third-party service failures (e.g., card networks, DNS providers)
- Issues caused by Customer's internet connectivity

### 1.4 Uptime Credits

If Maple fails to meet the uptime commitment, Customer is eligible for service credits:

**Starter/Growth Tier:**
- 99.0% - 99.49% uptime: 5% monthly fee credit
- 98.5% - 98.99% uptime: 15% monthly fee credit
- Below 98.5% uptime: 25% monthly fee credit

**Enterprise Tier:**
- 99.5% - 99.94% uptime: 15% monthly fee credit
- 99.0% - 99.49% uptime: 30% monthly fee credit
- 98.5% - 98.99% uptime: 50% monthly fee credit
- Below 98.5% uptime: 75% monthly fee credit

---

## 2. SUPPORT RESPONSE TIMES

### 2.1 Priority Definitions

**P0 - Critical (Service Down)**
- Complete platform unavailability
- Payment processing fully blocked
- Data breach or security incident
- Transaction processing success rate below 90%

**P1 - High (Major Impact)**
- Significant feature degradation
- Payment processing severely impaired (success rate 90-95%)
- Critical API endpoint failing
- Affects substantial portion of transactions

**P2 - Medium (Moderate Impact)**
- Partial feature degradation with workaround available
- Non-critical API issues
- Report generation delays
- Individual user access issues

**P3 - Low (Minor Impact)**
- Cosmetic issues
- Feature requests
- General questions
- Documentation clarifications

### 2.2 Response Time Commitments

**Starter Tier:**
| Priority | Initial Response | Business Hours |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| P0 | 30 minutes | 24/7 |
| P1 | 4 hours | Business hours |
| P2 | 1 business day | Business hours |
| P3 | 2 business days | Business hours |

**Growth Tier:**
| Priority | Initial Response | Business Hours |
|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| P0 | 30 minutes | 24/7 |
| P1 | 2 hours | Business hours |
| P2 | 8 hours | Business hours |
| P3 | 1 business day | Business hours |

**Enterprise Tier:**
| Priority | Initial Response | Resolution Target | Business Hours |
|----------|-----------------|-------------------|----------------|
| P0 | 10 minutes | 2 hours | 24/7 |
| P1 | 30 minutes | 12 hours | 24/7 |
| P2 | 2 hours | 2 business days | Business hours |
| P3 | 4 hours | 3 business days | Business hours |

### 2.3 Business Hours Definition

**Business Hours:** Monday - Friday, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM in Customer's primary business timezone, excluding recognized US federal holidays.

**Enterprise 24/7:** P0 and P1 support available around the clock for Enterprise customers.
